# Page 1: Overview

## Company Overview
Tata Motors Limited is a prominent player in the automotive industry operating under Tata Group's umbrella and headquartered at Tolstoy Marg, Mumbai-400009 (Mumbai), India. As one of India's largest publicly listed companies by market capitalization since its inception as TELCO Ltd on September 9th, 1945, it has evolved into an integrated diversified enterprise with a rich heritage in the automotive sector.

The company operates through various subsidiaries including Tata Motors Limited and Daimler AG under TATA SENSYS. With a significant presence across several markets like India (with brands Maruti Suzuki, MG Motor), Mexico (Fiat Chrysler Automobiles as FCA-Mexico), China (Great Wall Honda Corporation; Great Wall Motors Company Limited - GWMCL), South Korea (Tata Hyundai Group JV under Tata Advanced Automotive Systems Inc.), and North America (through Ford's Special Purpose Vehicle – SPV in the US, Jaguar Land Rover through Jaguar Land Rover SpV LLC Canada). The company has shown a steady commitment to innovation with numerous advancements including India's first diesel-hybrid car—the Maruti Suzuki Celerio Diesel Hybrid—and Tata Harrier.

## ESG Rating Overview
Tata Motors Limited's Environmental Social Governance (ESG) rating showcases an A+ in the current year and was rated as 'A' for previous years. The company's impressive net worth of 320.41 billion INR coupled with a turnover exceeding seven hundred five-billion reflects robust financial health, enabling continued investment into sustainable practices across all business functions.

Tata Motors maintains strong governance standards that remain elevated due to its high E score (85) and S Score (72), alongside rigorous Corporate Social Responsibility activities which are obligatory given the CSR Applicable status. Additionally, despite a female representation percentage of only 9.03%, Tata's overall ESG performance is commendable.
